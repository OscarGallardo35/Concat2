# VibeCut trademark policy

The VibeCut **source code** is free software under AGPL-3.0-or-later. The VibeCut
**name and logo** are not part of that grant.

This is deliberate and it is normal: the AGPL, like every GNU licence, conveys
no trademark rights (see AGPL-3.0 section 7(e)). Mozilla, Docker and the Rust
project all separate the two the same way. The code is yours to fork; the name
identifies builds that come from this project, so that a user downloading
"VibeCut" knows what they are getting.

## The marks

- The word mark **VibeCut**, as the name of this video editor.
- The VibeCut logo and its variants, in [`assets/`](assets/).

## What you may do without asking

- **Say what your software is.** Nominative use is always fine: "a plugin for
  VibeCut", "compatible with VibeCut", "a fork of VibeCut", "imports VibeCut
  projects". Describe the relationship accurately and you need no permission.
- **Redistribute official builds** under the name VibeCut, unmodified.
- **Package VibeCut for a distribution** (Nix, Homebrew, AUR, Debian, Flatpak…)
  from unmodified upstream source, keeping the name. Patches confined to
  packaging, paths and build flags are fine.
- **Write about VibeCut.** Reviews, tutorials, videos, screenshots, comparisons,
  academic work. No permission needed, no approval sought.
- **Use the logo** to link to or refer to this project.

## What needs a different name

If you distribute a **modified** VibeCut, please give it your own name and your
own icon. Concretely, rename before you ship if you:

- change the editor's behaviour, engine, defaults or bundled models;
- add or remove features and distribute the result to others;
- ship builds users could mistake for official ones.

You keep every right the AGPL grants you: fork it, change it, sell it, host
it. Just call it something else, so bug reports and reputations land in the
right place. Forks are welcome here; that is the point of the licence.

## What is not allowed

- Presenting a modified build as official VibeCut.
- Using **VibeCut** or a confusingly similar name in your product name, company
  name, app-store listing, or domain in a way that implies this project
  endorses or maintains it.
- Using the logo as your own product's icon or branding.
- Registering the VibeCut mark, or a confusingly similar one, in any
  jurisdiction.

## Upstream

VibeCut is a fork of [Concat](https://github.com/jub0t/Concat), and it is not
affiliated with, endorsed by or maintained by the Concat project or its author.
The Concat name and logo are theirs, which is exactly why this fork ships under
its own name and icon: to keep the two projects, their bug reports and their
reputations apart. The AGPL requires that the source keep Concat's copyright
lines, and it does.

Bug reports about VibeCut go to
[this repository's issues](https://github.com/OscarGallardo35/VibeCut/issues),
never upstream.

permitted when made.*
